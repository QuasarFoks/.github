# Hello, we are QuasarFoks! 👋

**An open community of enthusiasts.**

**Language**: *English* | [Русский](README.md)

Our mission is to make technology more accessible, simpler, and understandable for everyone.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### 🏢 Are you a commercial organization?
No. We are a non-profit community of like-minded people who want to make the digital world better and more open.

### 🔒 Do you collect user data?
Absolutely not. This is our fundamental principle.

In our products, you won't find:
*   **Advertising**
*   **Telemetry**
*   **Error and log collection**
*   **Hidden processes**

We strictly follow the rule: users must be confident that the program is not tracking them. Sometimes fighting this is challenging, but we don't compromise.

### 💬 How does communication work?
We're always happy to have live communication with the community! For us, your voice is more valuable than any money.

*   **Your opinion matters:** Don't like something? Your voice will be heard and considered.
*   **We make decisions together:** Key decisions are often made based on community opinion and voting.

### 🤝 Collaboration
We're open to collaborations and joint projects! The main condition is that everything must remain transparent and open.

## 💪 How to help?

We don't need donations. The best help is your participation!

We would be incredibly happy with:
*   **Code:** Fork any project you like and suggest your improvements.
*   **Testing:** Help find bugs and inaccuracies.
*   **Feedback:** Even simple feedback or ideas mean a lot to us!

**The process is simple:**
1.  Fork the project.
2.  Do what you think is necessary.
3.  Create a Pull Request - and your changes will go through fast and fair moderation.
